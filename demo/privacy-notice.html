Translation

The language selector in the footer is optional and stays switched off until you allow translation under “Cookie preferences”. Nothing is loaded from outside this website before that. Once allowed, your browser loads a script from GTranslate (cdn.gtranslate.net) and the text of the page is sent to Google’s translation service to be translated: into the language you choose, or automatically into your browser’s language when it is one of those offered. These services receive your IP address and browser details and may process them outside your country; their own privacy policies apply. Your choice is remembered in your browser (see below), so the pages you open next are translated, and their text sent to Google, in the same way.

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not set cookies for visitors. It stores a few small items in your browser, none of which identify you:

  • eds-consent:elite-nail-studio (local storage, this website): remembers your privacy choice.
  • wpEmojiSettingsSupports (session storage, WordPress): records whether your browser can display emoji. It is removed when you close the tab.
  • __GT_TRANSLATE_LANGS (local storage, GTranslate): created only after you allow translation, when a page is translated. It stores the original and the chosen language, so that the next pages are translated too.
  • gt_autoswitch (local storage, GTranslate): records that your browser’s language has been checked, so the automatic switch happens only once.
  • eds-dps:elite-nail-studio (local storage, demonstration sites only): remembers the colour palette you previewed with the “Try Colors” tool. It contains only the palette’s name, never leaves your browser, and is removed when you choose “Reset to Default”.

You can delete these items at any time in your browser settings. Staff who log in to manage the website also receive WordPress login cookies. The Cookie Policy describes each item and how to change your choices.
